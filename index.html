Your tech, auto & home guy

Built with Ronald.

I'm Ronald. I help people and small businesses with technology, common vehicle repairs and practical work around the home. From a brake job or a room that needs paint to a computer repair or custom app, start by telling me what you need working.

Can we work locally or remotely?

Local drop-off, vehicle work and on-site home visits in Chicago's south suburbs are arranged after we connect. Remote support, website and software requests can begin here too. Tell me where you are and what you need so we can choose a practical way to work together.

What if I don't know what's wrong?

You don't need a diagnosis. Describe what happens, when it started and the device or system involved. Choose "Not sure yet" on the request form if you don't know which service fits.

How do scope and pricing work?

Start with a service request. I'll review the problem, discuss the options and confirm the scope and price with you before paid work moves forward.
